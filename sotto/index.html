Sotto

Local Romanian and English dictation for macOS. Double Fn starts recording, one Fn stops, and the transcript is pasted into the focused app.

Source on GitHub

macOS

How it works

Press Fn twice to start recording and Fn once to stop. Sotto transcribes the audio on the Mac, in Romanian or English, and pastes the text into whatever app has focus. No account, no server, no audio leaving the machine.

Recognition runs on one of two local engines: Parakeet through FluidAudio, or Whisper through whisper.cpp. The whole path: Fn twice to record, Parakeet or Whisper on the Mac, paste.

While it records, the notch on the MacBook becomes a pill with twelve bars that follow the microphone level. That pill is drawn by Eclipse, which Sotto reaches over a local socket; without Eclipse, Sotto shows its own recorder at the bottom of the screen.

A personal tool, built and used on one MacBook Air. The only screenshot that exists is the cover above.

Credit and license

Sotto is a modified version of VoiceInk v2.21 by Prakash Joshi, released under GPL v3. Sotto is GPL v3 too.

Local engines and libraries: FluidAudio (Apache 2.0) runs Parakeet (CC BY 4.0), whisper.cpp (MIT) runs Whisper.
