How it is built

Everything runs in Docker Compose on Linux: a React front end, a Node.js and Express API, and MongoDB. Gmail access goes through OAuth 2.0. The AI part of each verdict comes from a model served by Ollama.

Mail comes in from Gmail, Barb runs its rules and asks the Ollama model, and the two together give the verdict.

It started as my bachelor thesis at Politehnica University Timișoara under the name SecureInbox, graded 8.9/10 in July 2026, and kept going as Barb.
